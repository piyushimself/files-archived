A Curated Reading Journey

50 Manga That Will
Move You

From your first page · to stories you'll never forget

I

Start Here — Easy & Joyful

BEGINNER FRIENDLY · LIGHT EMOTIONAL WEIGHT · PERFECT GATEWAY

No. 01
Yotsuba&!
よつばと！
Slice of Life Wholesome Ongoing
A curious 5-year-old discovers the world for the first time. Pure joy, zero plot pressure. The easiest manga you'll ever love.
🌿
No. 02
Chi's Sweet Home
チーズスイートホーム
Comedy Cozy Complete
A lost kitten named Chi finds a family. Short chapters, simple art, and a warmth that lingers. Impossible not to smile.
🐱
No. 03
Dungeon Meshi
ダンジョン飯
Fantasy Funny & Deep Complete
Adventurers cook and eat monsters in a dungeon. Sounds absurd — becomes surprisingly profound about life, food, and survival.
🍖
No. 04
My Hero Academia
僕のヒーローアカデミア
Shonen Action Inspiring Complete
A boy born without powers in a world of superheroes refuses to give up. Energetic, warm, and incredibly easy to get hooked on.
⚡
No. 05
One Piece
ワンピース
Adventure Epic & Funny Ongoing
The greatest adventure in manga. A boy who can stretch like rubber sets out to become King of the Pirates. Endlessly creative and surprisingly emotional.
⚓
No. 06
Dr. Stone
ドクターストーン
Sci-Fi Adventure Exciting Complete
All of humanity is petrified for 3,700 years. One genius boy rebuilds civilization using science. Unique, clever, and hugely fun.
🧪
No. 07
Spy × Family
スパイファミリー
Comedy Hilarious & Sweet Ongoing
A spy, an assassin, and a telepathic girl pretend to be a family. None of them know each other's secrets. Brilliantly funny with genuine heart.
🕵️
No. 08
Kaoru Hana wa Rin to Saku
香る花は凛と咲く
Romance Sweet Ongoing
A quiet, slow romance between two students at neighboring schools. Gentle and beautifully drawn — perfect first romance manga.
🌸
· · ·
II

Getting Deeper — Stories with Heart

EMOTIONAL · CHARACTER-DRIVEN · SOME TEARS AHEAD

No. 09
A Silent Voice
聲の形
Drama Redemption Complete
A former bully seeks forgiveness from a deaf girl he once tormented. Honest, painful, and one of the most moving stories about human connection.
💙
No. 10
Your Lie in April
四月は君の嘘
Romance / Music Bittersweet Complete
A pianist who can no longer hear his own music meets a violinist who changes everything. Beautiful and heartbreaking in equal measure.
🎹
No. 11
Fruits Basket
フルーツバスケット
Romance / Fantasy Healing Complete
A kind girl lives with a cursed family who transform into zodiac animals when hugged. Deeply emotional story about trauma, acceptance, and love.
🍊
No. 12
March Comes in Like a Lion
3月のライオン
Slice of Life Melancholic Ongoing
A teenage shogi prodigy battles loneliness and depression. Slow, tender, and achingly real — about finding warmth in unexpected places.
♟️
No. 13
Barakamon
ばらかもん
Slice of Life Healing & Funny Complete
A city calligrapher exiled to a rural island rediscovers himself through its simple-hearted villagers. Warm, funny, quietly wise.
🖌️
No. 14
Haikyuu!!
ハイキュー！！
Sports Thrilling & Moving Complete
High school volleyball. A short boy with an enormous heart fights for every point. The best sports manga ever made — guaranteed tears by the end.
🏐
No. 15
Blue Period
ブルーピリオド
Art / Drama Passionate Ongoing
A straight-A student with no passion discovers painting and falls completely. About obsession, craft, and the terrifying joy of making art.
🎨
No. 16
Solanin
ソラニン
Josei / Music Nostalgic & Sad Complete
Twenty-somethings in Tokyo try to figure out what to do with their lives. Inio Asano's most accessible work — gentle, realistic, and quietly devastating.
🎸
No. 17
Orange
オレンジ
Romance / Sci-Fi Tearjerker Complete
A girl receives letters from her future self, warning her to save a classmate from his fate. About regret, love, and the courage to act.
🍊
No. 18
Nana
ナナ
Josei / Drama Intense & Real On Hiatus
Two women named Nana share an apartment in Tokyo, living completely different lives. About friendship, love, and dreams — a masterpiece of emotional honesty.
🖤
· · ·
III

Epic Journeys — Long-Form Wonders

IMMERSIVE · WORLD-BUILDING · YEARS OF READING AHEAD

No. 19
Fullmetal Alchemist
鋼の錬金術師
Fantasy / Action Epic Complete
Two brothers lose their bodies pursuing forbidden alchemy. A perfect story — thrilling, philosophically rich, and emotionally complete. One of the all-time greats.
⚗️
No. 20
Berserk
ベルセルク
Dark Fantasy Devastating Ongoing
The story of Guts — a man born into suffering who fights against a fate designed to break him. Brutal, beautiful, and unlike anything else in fiction.
⚔️
No. 21
Attack on Titan
進撃の巨人
Dark Action Shocking Complete
Humanity hides behind walls from giant humanoid monsters. What begins as survival horror becomes one of manga's most complex political narratives.
🧱
No. 22
Hunter × Hunter
HUNTER×HUNTER
Adventure / Battle Unpredictable Ongoing (Hiatus)
A boy searches for his absent hunter father. Relentlessly subversive — every arc reinvents itself. One of the most intelligent shonen ever written.
🦋
No. 23
Vinland Saga
ヴィンランド・サガ
Historical Profound Complete
A Viking warrior's son is consumed by revenge — until he questions if violence was ever the answer. A grand epic about war, peace, and what a true warrior is.
🛡️
No. 24
Vagabond
バガボンド
Historical / Samurai Philosophical On Hiatus
The fictionalized life of Miyamoto Musashi, Japan's greatest swordsman. Breathtaking artwork. A meditation on strength, emptiness, and what it means to truly live.
🌾
No. 25
Naruto
ナルト
Ninja / Action Nostalgic Complete
An orphaned outcast dreams of becoming Hokage. A generation-defining epic about loneliness, friendship, and never giving up — with one of manga's greatest rivalries.
🌀
No. 26
Kingdom
キングダム
Historical War Heroic Ongoing
A slave boy fights to become China's greatest general during the Warring States period. Massive, gripping, and deeply moving in its portrayal of leadership and loyalty.
🏯
· · ·
IV

Quiet Masterworks — Literary Manga

SLOW-BURN · ARTISTIC · DEEPLY MOVING IN SUBTLE WAYS

No. 27
Mushishi
蟲師
Fantasy / Folklore Haunting Complete
A wanderer studies mysterious life forms that blur the line between life and death. Anthology-style — each story a quiet poem about nature, loss, and the invisible world.
🌫️
No. 28
Biomega
バイオメガ
Sci-Fi Atmospheric Complete
A synthetic human traverses a dying Earth in a post-apocalyptic world overrun by a zombie virus. Dense, cinematic, visually extraordinary.
🤖
No. 29
Goodnight Punpun
おやすみプンプン
Psychological Drama Devastating Complete
A boy depicted as a cartoon bird grows up in a broken world. Brutally honest about depression and self-destruction. One of the most affecting coming-of-age stories ever told — not for beginners.
🐤
No. 30
Aria
ARIA
Sci-Fi / Slice of Life Serene Complete
Gondoliers on a terraformed Mars-Venice. Slow, peaceful, and quietly transcendent — the most relaxing manga ever written. A masterpiece of "iyashikei" (healing) storytelling.
🌊
No. 31
The Flowers of Evil
惡の華
Psychological Uncomfortable & Real Complete
A middle-school boy is blackmailed by a classmate who considers them both "deviant." A uniquely unsettling coming-of-age story drawn in photorealistic style.
🌹
No. 32
Planetes
プラネテス
Sci-Fi / Drama Thoughtful Complete
Space debris collectors in the near future. A quiet, grounded meditation on dreams, work, relationships, and what we owe each other in a fragile world.
🛸
No. 33
A Bride's Story
乙嫁語り
Historical Gentle & Rich Ongoing
Life along the Silk Road in 19th-century Central Asia. Kaoru Mori's art is so detailed it feels like a historical artifact. Slow, intimate, extraordinary.
🏺
No. 34
I Am a Hero
アイアムアヒーロー
Horror / Survival Tense & Sad Complete
A manga artist with no confidence survives a zombie apocalypse. A psychological portrait of inadequacy and unlikely heroism told through body horror.
🔫
· · ·
V

Crime, Thriller & Suspense

PSYCHOLOGICAL · GRIPPING · IMPOSSIBLE TO PUT DOWN

No. 35
Monster
モンスター
Psychological Thriller Gripping Complete
A German surgeon saves a boy's life — only to discover he became a serial killer. A decade-spanning hunt through Europe. The greatest thriller manga ever made.
👁️
No. 36
Death Note
デスノート
Psychological Thriller Edge-of-Seat Complete
A teenager finds a notebook that kills anyone whose name is written in it. A cat-and-mouse game of pure intellect that raises real questions about justice.
📓
No. 37
20th Century Boys
20世紀少年
Mystery / Sci-Fi Epic Mystery Complete
A childhood promise becomes a global conspiracy. Naoki Urasawa weaves a sprawling mystery across decades — about memory, friendship, and the stories we tell ourselves.
☢️
No. 38
Pluto
PLUTO
Sci-Fi Mystery Emotional & Cerebral Complete
Urasawa reimagines Astro Boy as a murder mystery about robot rights and the nature of hatred. Profound, moving, and a masterpiece of manga storytelling.
🤖
No. 39
Billy Bat
ビリーバット
Historical Thriller Mind-Bending Complete
A cartoon bat symbol appears throughout history — from the moon landing to JFK's assassination. A conspiracy thriller unlike anything else. Urasawa at his most ambitious.
🦇
· · ·
VI

Genre Gems — Hidden & Essential

VARIED · UNDERRATED · EACH UNLIKE THE OTHERS

No. 40
Yokohama Kaidashi Kikou
ヨコハマ買い出し紀行
Post-Apocalyptic / Calm Melancholy & Peace Complete
A robot runs a coffee shop at the edge of a slowly sinking world. A beautiful, elegiac manga about appreciating existence as it fades. Uniquely moving.
☕
No. 41
Homunculus
ホムンクルス
Psychological Disturbing & Deep Complete
A man undergoes trepanation and gains the ability to see people's trauma as distorted forms. A disturbing but deeply empathetic exploration of hidden pain.
🧠
No. 42
Real
リアル
Sports / Drama Raw & Honest Ongoing
Wheelchair basketball. Three young men deal with disability, guilt, and the fight to reclaim their lives. Takehiko Inoue's most honest, devastating work.
🏀
No. 43
Slam Dunk
スラムダンク
Sports Exhilarating Complete
A delinquent joins basketball to impress a girl — and discovers the game of his life. The definitive sports manga; its final game is still talked about decades later.
🏀
No. 44
Blame!
ブラム！
Sci-Fi / Architecture Awe-Inspiring Complete
A silent wanderer journeys through a city that has grown to span the known universe. Almost wordless — pure visual storytelling on a cosmic scale. Unlike anything else.
🌆
No. 45
Kaguya-sama: Love is War
かぐや様は告らせたい
Romance / Comedy Hilarious & Sweet Complete
Two genius student council members refuse to confess — whoever falls first loses. A brilliant comedy that sneaks in enormous emotional depth when you least expect it.
💝
No. 46
Onanie Master Kurosawa
オナニーマスター黒沢
Drama Surprisingly Moving Complete
Deceptive title, deeply earnest story. A loner boy is blackmailed into helping a class outcast — and both are completely changed. Raw coming-of-age storytelling.
📝
No. 47
The Way of the Househusband
極主夫道
Comedy Pure Fun Ongoing
Japan's most feared yakuza boss retires to become a stay-at-home husband. Every chapter is a perfect comedy sketch. Impossible not to laugh out loud.
🧹

VII

The Final Three — Essential Summits

LIFE-CHANGING · READ THESE BEFORE YOU DIE

No. 48
Nausicaä of the Valley of the Wind
風の谷のナウシカ
Sci-Fi / Ecology Transcendent Complete
Miyazaki's manga is far darker and deeper than the film. A young princess navigates a dying world of warring civilizations and toxic jungles. The most visionary manga ever drawn.
🌬️
No. 49
Dororo
どろろ
Historical / Dark Fantasy Haunting Complete
A samurai born without limbs — sacrificed to demons by his father — fights to reclaim his body one demon at a time. Tezuka's most haunting, soulful masterwork.
🌑
No. 50
Phoenix
火の鳥
Mythology / Sci-Fi Cosmic & Eternal Incomplete (Tezuka died)
Osamu Tezuka's life work. An anthology tracing humanity across past and future through the immortal Phoenix. Each arc stands alone. Together, they form manga's greatest meditation on life and death.
🔥
