How to use Coursera for free

When you click "Enroll" on any Coursera course, a popup appears. Look for "Audit this course" — it's a small link, usually at the bottom of the popup. Clicking it gives you full access to all video lectures and reading material, completely free. You only pay if you want the certificate.
