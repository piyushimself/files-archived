Poetry & writing

Poetry, writing prompts and small writing surfaces belong together: one helps you read language, another helps you practise it, and the tools give you somewhere to keep the work.

A useful way to approach this section

You do not need to begin at the first prompt or finish a whole collection. Choose one page, one poem or one exercise. Keep drafts small and return to them when you have something to add.
