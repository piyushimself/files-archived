STORIES, CURIOSITY AND LEARNING TOGETHER

Children & family learning

A family learning shelf should make age, stage and purpose clear. These guides gather stories from different regions, reading sequences for young children and wider creative and educational resources.

A useful way to approach this section

Use the age band and reading stage as a guide, not a rigid rule. Read aloud, follow the child's attention, and revisit favourites. For large directories, choose one theme or country at a time rather than trying to cover everything.
