LITERATURE, FILM CRAFT AND LIFELONG READING

Books & reading journeys

Reading lists are more useful when they explain why a work belongs, where it comes from and what kind of journey it supports. This section brings together world fiction, Asian literature, books for filmmakers and large personal reference collections.

A useful way to approach this section

Use the curated paths as a sequence, not a compulsory syllabus. Read across traditions and periods; pause to follow authors or themes that interest you. The larger reference collections are for discovery rather than completion.

Suggested routes

Follow one of these small paths instead of trying to browse everything at once.

Begin with a broad world-fiction reference, then follow a regional or thematic route.
