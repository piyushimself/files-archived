Anime, manga & comics

From welcoming first reads to complex manga, anime adaptations, political comics and reflective curricula. The collection is arranged so readers can choose by mood, depth, form and subject rather than face one enormous undifferentiated list.

A useful way to approach this section

Start with the beginner guide or recommendations if you are new to the medium. Use the larger indexes when you want breadth. The darker and mature collections are separated from the general reading paths and clearly labelled.

Follow one of these small paths instead of trying to browse everything at once.
