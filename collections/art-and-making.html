Art & creative practice

Practical learning resources sit alongside artist biographies and small creative exercises. This section is meant to help you move from browsing references to actually practising a medium.

A useful way to approach this section

Pick one medium and one manageable exercise. Use the free resource directories for instruction, the artist guides for visual understanding, and the journals or generators for a regular low-pressure practice.
