THINKING, ATTENTION, EMOTION AND THE SHAPE OF A LIFE

Ideas & reflection

These collections are designed for slower engagement: reflective prompts, thinkers, films and books that help examine attention, identity, grief, mortality, wonder and the choices that shape a life.

A useful way to approach this section

Choose one theme that feels relevant now. Work through a short sequence, write a few notes, and leave room between items. These are invitations to reflect, not clinical advice or a productivity programme.
