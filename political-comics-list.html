🇩🇪 Germany & Europe — WWI, WWII & The Holocaust
🇺🇸
Western USA
Maus
Art Spiegelman
1980 – 1991
Pulitzer Prize-winning account of the Holocaust told through a father-son interview, with Jews as mice and Nazis as cats. The first masterpiece in comic book history.
HolocaustWWIIGermanyMemoir
🇩🇪
Western Germany
Irmina
Barbara Yelin
2014
A young German woman moves to London in the mid-1930s — a personal-scale story of how ordinary people became complicit in Nazism.
Nazi GermanyComplicity1930s
🇫🇷
Bande Dessinée France
It Was the War of the Trenches
Jacques Tardi
1993
A brutal anti-war account of WWI from a French soldier's perspective. Tardi is France's premier war comics artist.
WWIFranceAnti-War
🇫🇷
Bande Dessinée France
Goddamn This War!
Jacques Tardi
2012
A companion volume to Trenches — relentless, unromantic trench warfare depicted with documentary fury.
WWIFranceAnti-War
🇮🇷 Iran — Islamic Revolution
🇮🇷
Bande Dessinée Iran / France
Persepolis
Marjane Satrapi
2000 – 2003
Memoir of growing up in Iran during the Islamic Revolution — funny, heartbreaking, essential. Covers the fall of the Shah, the Iran-Iraq War, and life under theocracy.
IranRevolutionMemoirIslam
🇯🇵 Japan — WWII & Atomic Bomb
🇯🇵
Manga Japan
Barefoot Gen
Keiji Nakazawa
1973 – 1987
A survivor's account of the Hiroshima atomic bombing. Nakazawa critiques both the U.S. and Japanese governments that propagated the war. One of manga's defining political works.
HiroshimaAtomic BombWWIIJapan
🇯🇵
Manga Japan
Onward Towards Our Noble Deaths
Shigeru Mizuki
1973
Semi-autobiographical manga about Japanese soldiers in the Pacific theater — deeply critical of Imperial Japan's "honorable death" ideology and military command.
WWIIPacific WarAnti-Militarism
🇯🇵
Manga Japan
In This Corner of the World
Fumiyo Kouno
2007 – 2009
A quiet, domestic story of a young woman in Hiroshima Prefecture during WWII. Won the Japan Media Arts Award. Civilian life under wartime policy, told with devastating gentleness.
WWIIHiroshimaCivilian Life
🇺🇸 United States — Civil Rights & American Politics
🇺🇸
Western USA
March (Trilogy)
John Lewis & Andrew Aydin
2013 – 2016
A firsthand account of the Civil Rights Movement by Congressman John Lewis, who marched alongside Martin Luther King Jr. Essential American political history.
Civil RightsUSAMemoir1960s
🇺🇸
Western USA
Kent State
Derf Backderf
2020
The 1970 National Guard massacre of student protesters at Kent State University. Meticulously researched. A defining moment in American anti-war protest history.
USAProtestVietnam EraState Violence
🇺🇸
Western USA
They Called Us Enemy
George Takei
2019
Star Trek's George Takei recounts his childhood in Japanese-American internment camps during WWII. Racism and wartime civil rights violations from a survivor's perspective.
USAInternmentWWIIMemoir
🇺🇸
Western USA
The Harlem Hellfighters
Max Brooks & Caanan White
2014
The true story of the all-Black 369th Infantry Regiment in WWI, who fought longer than any other American unit and returned home to a nation that still denied them rights.
WWIUSARaceMilitary
🇫🇷 France — Revolution & Empire
🇫🇷
Manga Japan / France
The Rose of Versailles
Riyoko Ikeda
1972 – 1973
Marie Antoinette and the complex politics of the French Revolution. Considered the first shojo manga to achieve mainstream critical and commercial success. Revolution through personal drama.
French RevolutionMarie AntoinetteMonarchyShojo
🇫🇷
Manga Japan / France
Innocent
Shinichi Sakamoto
2013 – 2016
The French Revolution through the eyes of the Sanson family — the official executioners of Paris. Stunning art, morally complex. Explores justice, power, and guilt in the guillotine era.
French RevolutionExecutionJustice
🇨🇳 China — Ancient History & Warring States
🇨🇳
Manga Japan / China
Kingdom
Yasuhisa Hara
2006 – present
Based on true events from China's Warring States period — the rise of Qin and its unification of China. Depicts in-depth national politics and diplomacy between nations. Massive ongoing epic.
Ancient ChinaWarring StatesUnificationPolitics
🇨🇳
Manhua Hong Kong
Ravages of Time
Chan Mou
2001 – present
A retelling of the Three Kingdoms period — one of Chinese history's most defining political eras. Incredible tactical and political depth. One of Hong Kong's landmark comics.
Three KingdomsChinaStrategyPolitics
🇵🇸 Palestine / Israel — Occupation & Conflict
🇵🇸
Western Malta / USA
Palestine
Joe Sacco
1993 – 1996
On-the-ground journalism comics from the occupied territories in the early 1990s. Raw, award-winning reportage that established Sacco as the pioneer of comics journalism.
PalestineOccupationJournalism1990s
🇵🇸
Western Malta / USA
Footnotes in Gaza
Joe Sacco
2009
Investigates a 1956 massacre in Gaza that history largely forgot. Deep, meticulous journalism — Sacco at his most thorough and devastating.
GazaMassacre1956Journalism
🇸🇾
Bande Dessinée France / Libya / Syria
The Arab of the Future (Series)
Riad Sattouf
2014 – 2022
A memoir series about growing up in Gaddafi's Libya and Assad's Syria as a half-French, half-Arab child. Brilliant, disorienting, and deeply revealing about Arab authoritarian states.
LibyaSyriaMemoirDictatorship
🌍
Western Middle East
Meanwhile, in the Middle East...
Yazan Al-Saadi & Various Artists
2017
Short comics documenting political and social unrest in Lebanon, Egypt, Yemen, Palestine, Sudan, and Bahrain during the 2010s Arab Spring and its aftermath.
Arab SpringLebanonEgyptYemen
🌍 Africa — Colonial & Post-Colonial History
🇬🇭
Western USA / Ghana
Abina and the Important Men
Trevor Getz & Liz Clarke
2011
Based on a real 1876 court transcript of an enslaved Ghanaian woman who sued her master in British colonial court. Winner of the 2014 James Harvey Robinson Prize.
GhanaColonialismSlavery1870s
🇨🇩
Bande Dessinée Belgium / Congo
Madame Livingstone: The Great War in the Congo
Christophe Cassiau-Haurie
2012
Based on the true story of an unlikely Belgian-African partnership responsible for sinking a German battleship in the Congo during WWI. Rare African WWI perspective.
CongoWWIColonialismAfrica
🇹🇳 Tunisia — Arab Spring 2011
🇹🇳
Western Tunisia
After the Spring
Various
2016
Four young Tunisians cope with the uncertain future following the Arab Spring of 2011. One of the most direct graphic accounts of life after revolution — what happens after the cameras leave.
TunisiaArab Spring2011Revolution
🇮🇳 India — Independence, Kashmir & Emergency
🇮🇳
Western India
Munnu: A Boy from Kashmir
Malik Sajad
2015
A memoir-style comic about growing up in conflict-torn Kashmir. One of the only Kashmiri voices in graphic form — occupation, identity, and lost childhood.
KashmirIndiaOccupationMemoir
🇮🇳
Western India
Delhi Calm
Vishwajyoti Ghosh
2010
Covers India's Emergency period (1975–77) under Indira Gandhi — one of India's most controversial political crises, when civil liberties were suspended for nearly two years.
IndiaEmergency 1975Indira GandhiAuthoritarianism
🇦🇷 Latin America — Dictatorship
🇦🇷
Western Argentina
Eternaut (El Eternauta)
Héctor G. Oesterheld
1957 – 1959
Landmark Latin American comics — allegorical sci-fi doubling as a critique of Argentina's military dictatorship. Oesterheld himself was "disappeared" by the junta. A work that cost its creator his life.
ArgentinaDictatorshipDisappearedAllegory

Bande Dessinée Canada
Burma Chronicles

2007
Diary comics from Myanmar under military rule. An animator's journal inside a closed authoritarian state — accessible, wry, and quietly damning.
MyanmarMilitary RuleDiary
🇰🇵
Bande Dessinée Canada
Pyongyang
Guy Delisle
2003
An animator's journal inside North Korea — one of the only graphic accounts from inside the country. Chilling in its mundanity.

Paying the Land
Guy Delisle

Delisle in Jerusalem and the Palestinian territories — navigating checkpoints, settlements, and the daily reality of an occupied city. Deceptively calm, politically sharp.

⚖️ Totalitarianism & State Control
🇬🇧
Western UK
V for Vendetta
Alan Moore & David Lloyd
1982 – 1989
A dystopian future totalitarian England. An anarchist vigilante in a Guy Fawkes mask seeks to overthrow the government. Explores freedom, oppression, and the power of ideas. Written as a direct response to Thatcher's Britain.
TotalitarianismUKAnarchyThatcher Era
🇺🇸
Western UK / USA
Watchmen
Alan Moore & Dave Gibbons
1986 – 1987
Superheroes as instruments of state power. Set during Nixon's Cold War America — deconstructs American exceptionalism, nuclear anxiety, and unchecked authority. Possibly the most politically dense superhero story ever made.
Cold WarUSANixonNuclearPower
🌐
Western Brazil
1984 (Graphic Novel Adaptation)
Fido Nesti (after Orwell)
2021
The most acclaimed graphic adaptation of Orwell's surveillance state classic. Big Brother, doublethink, and the total erasure of individual thought — essential reading on propaganda and control.
TotalitarianismSurveillancePropagandaOrwell
🧱 Walls, Borders & Ethnic Persecution
🇯🇵
Manga Japan
Attack on Titan
Hajime Isayama
2009 – 2021
Takes inspiration from Germanic imagery, the Jewish Exodus, WWII, and the Holocaust. Covers war, fascism, antisemitism, racism, and social stratification. Deliberately multivalent — different readers see Israel/Palestine, Japan/Korea, or Taiwan/China.
FascismGenocideWallsEthnic Persecution
🇯🇵
Manga Japan
Nausicaä of the Valley of the Wind
Hayao Miyazaki
1982 – 1994
Post-apocalyptic world destroyed by pollution and war. Miyazaki used the medium to portray the futility of war and humanity's folly in controlling the environment. Anti-imperialism and ecological politics interwoven throughout.
Anti-WarEcologyImperialismPost-Apocalypse
⚗️ Militarism, Empire & State Violence
🇯🇵
Manga Japan
Fullmetal Alchemist
Hiromu Arakawa
2001 – 2010
The Ishvalan Civil War draws on imagery from the Iraq War — an ethnic group nearly wiped out by state policy. Widely read as a critique of capitalism and military dictatorship. One of manga's most morally rigorous political texts.
Military DictatorshipGenocideCapitalismIraq War Allegory
🇯🇵
Manga Japan
Akira
Katsuhiro Otomo
1982 – 1990
Set in Neo-Tokyo after a third World War. About youth radicalization, state military experiments on citizens, and institutional collapse. A direct response to Japan's postwar anxieties and the 1964 Tokyo Olympics.
Post-War JapanState PowerYouth RebellionCyberpunk
🇯🇵
Manga Japan
Vinland Saga
Makoto Yukimura
2005 – 2025
Vikings, conquest, and slavery — but at its core a pacifist argument. Explores how governments discard soldiers, the cycle of revenge and empire, and one man's rejection of warrior culture entirely.
VikingsSlaveryPacifismAnti-War
🏭 Class, Capitalism & Economic Power
🇫🇷
Bande Dessinée France
Snowpiercer
Jacques Lob & Jean-Marc Rochette
1982
Humanity's survivors on a train that endlessly circles a frozen Earth — wealthy in the front cars, starving in the back. Pure class warfare allegory, French bande dessinée at its most brutal. Predates the film by decades.
Class WarInequalityPost-ApocalypseFrance
🇺🇸
Western USA
Transmetropolitan
Warren Ellis & Darick Robertson
1997 – 2002
A gonzo journalist in a corrupt near-future America tears apart political hypocrisy, media manipulation, and voter apathy. Hunter S. Thompson energy applied to political satire. Frighteningly prescient.
SatireMediaCorruptionUSA
🇺🇸
Western USA
Y: The Last Man
Brian K. Vaughan & Pia Guerra
2002 – 2008
A plague wipes out every mammal with a Y chromosome. Explores politics, gender, power structures, and what actually holds civilization together — and what collapses when it's gone.
Gender PoliticsPower StructuresPost-Apocalypse
🤖 Technology, Surveillance & Posthuman Politics
🇯🇵
Manga Japan
Ghost in the Shell
Masamune Shirow
1989 – 1990
Set against political intrigue and cyber warfare, following a cyborg government operative hunting a hacker. Explores government overreach, state intelligence ethics, identity, and consciousness in a cyberpunk future.
SurveillanceCyberpunkIntelligenceIdentity
🇯🇵
Manga Japan
Pluto
Naoki Urasawa
2003 – 2009
A remake of Astro Boy's arc — reframed as a meditation on the War on Terror, WMDs, and what drives nations to conflict. Urasawa turns a children's story into a heartbreaking anti-war argument.
War on TerrorWMDAnti-WarRobot Rights
🌿 Colonialism & Indigenous Rights
🇯🇵
Manga Japan
Golden Kamuy
Satoru Noda
2014 – 2022
Set in 1907 Hokkaido — a Russo-Japanese War veteran and an Ainu girl search for gold ultimately meant to fund a sovereign Ainu state. Winner of the Manga Taishō and Tezuka Osamu Cultural Prize. One of manga's most serious treatments of indigenous erasure.
AinuIndigenous RightsColonialismJapan
🕵️ Power, Corruption & Political Conspiracy
🇯🇵
Manga Japan
20th Century Boys
Naoki Urasawa
1999 – 2006
A cult leader rises to global power using a childhood doomsday prophecy. A slow-burn thriller about how fascism grows from nostalgia, how cults of personality work, and how ordinary people fail to stop them until it's too late.
FascismCult of PersonalityConspiracyJapan
🇩🇪
Manga Japan / Germany
Monster
Naoki Urasawa
1994 – 2001
A German surgeon saves a child who becomes a serial killer with political ambitions. Set in post-reunification Germany and Eastern Europe — explores how Cold War-era trauma and political machinery create monsters, literally and figuratively.
GermanyCold WarPost-ReunificationTrauma
🇺🇸
Manga Japan / USA
Banana Fish
Akimi Yoshida
1985 – 1994
Street gangs, the CIA, drug trafficking, and political manipulation in 1980s New York. One of the earliest manga to engage seriously with American imperialism, Vietnam War trauma, and corrupt government operations.
CIAImperialismVietnam1980s USA
🌐 Speculative Futures & Systemic Critique
🇺🇸
Western USA
East of West
Jonathan Hickman & Nick Dragotta
2013 – 2019
Alternate America where the Civil War never ended and the country fragmented into competing ideological nations. Dense political world-building about ideology, religion, and national identity.
Alternate HistoryUSACivil WarIdeology
🇨🇦
Western Canada
We Stand on Guard
Brian K. Vaughan & Steve Skroce
2015
America invades Canada. A sharp satire about how easily the "freedom" rhetoric of empire applies to neighbors — told entirely from the invaded side. Subverts American reader expectations deliberately.
ImperialismCanadaUSAInvasion
🌌
Western USA
Saga
Brian K. Vaughan & Fiona Staples
2012 – present
Science fantasy on the surface — fundamentally about war profiteering, media propaganda, and what perpetuates cycles of conflict. One of the best depictions of civilians caught in geopolitical crossfire in all of comics.
War ProfiteeringPropagandaCiviliansGeopolitics
