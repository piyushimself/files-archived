I — Dignity & Empathy 25 works

"What does it mean to truly see another person?" These works ask you to hold strangers with tenderness — the disabled, the poor, the grieving, the despised.

Anime I·01
A Silent Voice
Koe no Katachi · Film · 2016
▸ Disability · Bullying · Redemption

A bully seeks forgiveness from the deaf girl he tormented. Reflect: How do I treat those who cannot defend themselves? Can I hold both guilt and the possibility of growth?

Manga I·02
A Silent Voice
Yoshitoki Ōima · 7 vols
▸ Disability · Communication · Self-worth

The manga goes deeper into the inner lives of every side character. Reflect: Every person around me has an interior world as complex as mine — am I curious about it?

Anime I·03
Violet Evergarden
Series + Film · 2018–2020
▸ Emotional Literacy · Trauma · Service

A war soldier learns to write letters for people who cannot express love. Reflect: What words do the people around me need to hear but never say?

Manga I·04
Vinland Saga
Makoto Yukimura · ongoing
▸ Vengeance vs. Compassion · War

A Viking warrior chooses farming over violence. Reflect: The strength to not retaliate — is this weakness or the deepest courage?

Anime I·05
Mushishi
Series · 2005
▸ Compassion · Non-judgment · Naturalism

A healer meets people afflicted by mysterious spirits, never judging, only helping. Reflect: Can I approach human suffering with the same calm, non-judgmental presence?

Manga I·06
Mushishi
Yuki Urushibara · 10 vols
▸ Stillness · Patience · Wonder

The manga's quieter pace deepens Ginko's acceptance of all living things. Reflect: What do I dismiss as "unimportant" that deserves more of my presence?

Anime I·07
Grave of the Fireflies
Isao Takahata · Film · 1988
▸ Civilian Suffering · War's Indifference

Two children survive — and don't — in post-war Japan. Reflect: Who suffers invisibly in large events that I consider history, not humanity?

Manga I·08
Barefoot Gen
Keiji Nakazawa · 10 vols
▸ Hiroshima · Survival · Human Cost of War

Autobiographical manga about surviving the atomic bomb. Reflect: What does it do to me to sit with historical horror through the eyes of a child?

Anime I·09
Natsume's Book of Friends
Series · 2008–ongoing
▸ Loneliness · Belonging · Kindness to Outsiders

A boy who sees spirits returns their names, freeing them from bondage. Reflect: Who around me is carrying something unspoken that a small act of recognition might release?

Manga I·10
Natsume's Book of Friends
Yuki Midorikawa · ongoing
▸ Inherited Burdens · Healing

The manga's slower rhythm shows how past wounds live in the body. Reflect: What did I inherit from my family that I carry without knowing?

Anime I·11
March Comes in Like a Lion
Sangatsu no Lion · Series · 2016
▸ Depression · Community · Being Seen

A lonely shogi prodigy is slowly drawn back into warmth by a family who simply includes him. Reflect: What does it mean to include someone, not fix them?

Manga I·12
March Comes in Like a Lion
Chica Umino · ongoing
▸ School Bullying · Caretaking Burden

A deeply honest arc about a girl being bullied, and the teacher who fails her first. Reflect: What is the cost of institutional silence in the face of cruelty?

Anime I·13
The Wind Rises
Miyazaki · Film · 2013
▸ Craft · Complicity · Beauty and Destruction

An engineer designs beautiful airplanes used for war. Reflect: Can talent be innocent of how it's used?

Manga I·14
I Am a Hero
Kengo Hanazawa · 22 vols
▸ Cowardice vs. Courage · Self-deception

A failed manga artist confronts a zombie apocalypse — and his own mediocrity. Reflect: Where in my life do I tell myself flattering stories that aren't true?

Anime I·15
Haibane Renmei
Series · 2002
▸ Guilt · Self-forgiveness · Community

Angel-like beings in a walled town carry unnamed sins. Reflect: What unresolved guilt do I carry that prevents me from being fully present?

Manga I·16
Real
Takehiko Inoue · ongoing
▸ Disability · Masculinity · Resilience

Three young men navigate basketball, disability, and broken pride. Reflect: How does my culture teach men to handle vulnerability — and is that healthy?

Anime I·17
Wolf Children
Mamoru Hosoda · Film · 2012
▸ Parental Sacrifice · Identity · Nature vs. Society

A mother raises two half-wolf children alone, letting them choose who to be. Reflect: What does truly unconditional love require of the one who gives it?

Manga I·18
Yotsuba&!
Kiyohiko Azuma · ongoing
▸ Wonder · Presence · Joy in the Ordinary

A small girl experiences the world with pure delight. Reflect: What would I notice today if I allowed myself Yotsuba's sense of wonder?

Anime I·19
My Neighbor Totoro
Miyazaki · Film · 1988
▸ Childhood Coping · Nature · Wonder

Two girls face their mother's illness through the language of imagination. Reflect: How do children cope with things too large to name? How do adults forget how?

Manga I·20
The Way of the Househusband
Kousuke Oono · ongoing
▸ Gender Roles · Domesticity · Kindness

A fierce yakuza becomes a devoted househusband. Reflect: What "masculine" roles do I treat as lesser? What care work do I overlook?

Anime I·21
Clannad: After Story
Series · 2008
▸ Family · Loss · Growing Up

A delinquent becomes a father and faces the hardest kind of love. Reflect: What did fatherhood or adulthood ask of the people who raised me that I never acknowledged?

Manga I·22
A Man and His Cat
Umi Sakurai · ongoing
▸ Loneliness · Simple Love · Elderly Care

An elderly widower adopts an unloved cat. Reflect: What unloved things — and people — in my world are waiting simply to be chosen?

Anime I·23
Spirited Away
Miyazaki · Film · 2001
▸ Labor · Greed · Keeping One's Name

A girl fights to remember her name in a world designed to make her forget. Reflect: What social and economic systems try to erase who I am? What name do I hold onto?

Manga I·24
Golden Kamuy
Satoru Noda · 31 vols
▸ Indigenous Culture · Colonialism · Survival

A Meiji-era adventure that lovingly documents Ainu culture. Reflect: What do I know of the cultures that existed before the ones I consider "normal"?

Anime I·25
Miss Hokusai
Film · 2015
▸ Women Artists · Family · Being Overlooked

The daughter of Hokusai, a brilliant artist herself, lives in her father's shadow. Reflect: Whose contributions are invisible in the stories we tell? Who taught whom?

II — Integrity Under Pressure 25 works

"Who are you when no one is watching — or when everyone is?" These works explore moral courage, complicity, and what we sacrifice to belong.

Anime II·01
Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood
Series · 2009
▸ Equivalent Exchange · State Power · Genocide

Two brothers uncover a genocide hidden beneath their nation's founding. Reflect: What comforts in my life rest on hidden violence or injustice I don't look at?

Manga II·02
Fullmetal Alchemist
Hiromu Arakawa · 27 vols
▸ Accountability · State Violence · Forgiveness

A manga with one of the most moral conclusions in all of fiction. Reflect: Is it possible to atone for structural violence? What does that even look like?

Anime II·03
Monster
Series · 2004
▸ Moral Absolutism · Evil's Origins · Compassion for Killers

A doctor saves a boy who becomes a serial killer and hunts him across Europe. Reflect: What made a person monstrous — and what is our duty to that story?

Manga II·04
Monster
Naoki Urasawa · 18 vols
▸ Nazi Psychology · Childhood Trauma · Conscience

The manga explores how a soul can be systematically destroyed. Reflect: What environments are destroying people's souls around me — and am I complicit?

Anime II·05
Death Note
Series · 2006
▸ Justice vs. Power · Self-righteousness · God Complex

A student with the power to kill anyone becomes the very thing he despised. Reflect: What do I do when I believe I am right? Where does righteousness become violence?

Manga II·06
Vagabond
Takehiko Inoue · 37 vols
▸ Ego · Mastery · The Violence of Ambition

Miyamoto Musashi chases invincibility and discovers it means nothing. Reflect: What am I trying to prove — and to whom?

Anime II·07
Vinland Saga
Series · 2019
▸ Revenge · Pacifism · True Strength

A warrior discovers that the path he chose destroyed him. Reflect: What have I sacrificed in pursuit of something that, if I got it, would not satisfy me?

Manga II·08
Berserk
Kentaro Miura · ongoing
▸ Betrayal · Dark Ambition · Survival of the Self

A warrior survives impossible cruelty and chooses to keep living. Reflect: What is the relationship between trauma and who I've become?

Anime II·09
Legend of the Galactic Heroes
Series · 1988
▸ Democracy · Autocracy · Political Ethics

Two military geniuses on opposite sides of a galactic civil war ask what governance is for. Reflect: Is order without freedom worth its price? Is freedom without order possible?

Manga II·10
20th Century Boys
Naoki Urasawa · 22 vols
▸ Cult Politics · Collective Memory · Resistance

A group of childhood friends fight a cult that's taken over the world. Reflect: How do charismatic figures build movements? When do I follow without thinking?

Anime II·11
Psycho-Pass
Series · 2012
▸ Surveillance · Preemptive Justice · Free Will

A society arrests people before they commit crimes. Reflect: When is safety used to justify control? Do I consent to being watched?

Manga II·12
Pluto
Naoki Urasawa · 8 vols
▸ War Crimes · Robot Ethics · Hatred's Origins

A reimagining of Astro Boy asks: what makes a human capable of genocide? Reflect: What is the seed of hatred — and when did it grow in me toward anyone?

Anime II·13
Barakamon
Series · 2014
▸ Humility · Creativity · Learning from Children

A calligrapher punishes a critic and is exiled to a rural island where children teach him what art is. Reflect: When did pride cost me something important? Who have I dismissed that I should have listened to?

Manga II·14
Blue Period
Tsubasa Yamaguchi · ongoing
▸ Passion vs. Practicality · Art · Honesty

A high-achieving student gives up everything to pursue painting. Reflect: What have I suppressed to appear "sensible"? What would I pursue if I stopped performing?

Anime II·15
Moriarty the Patriot
Series · 2020
▸ Class · Reform vs. Radicalism · The Price of Ideals

A noble dedicates his life to destroying Britain's class system — even if it costs him his soul. Reflect: Is it ethical to use wrong means for right ends?

Manga II·16
Dungeon Meshi (Delicious in Dungeon)
Ryoko Kui · 14 vols
▸ Resourcefulness · Ecological Ethics · Food

Adventurers eat the monsters they kill to survive. Reflect: What is my relationship to the food chain? To resourcefulness?

Anime II·17
Princess Mononoke
Miyazaki · Film · 1997
▸ Progress vs. Nature · No True Villain · Industrial Ethics

Neither the humans nor the forest gods are wrong — only the system. Reflect: Where do I participate in "development" that destroys something irreplaceable?

Manga II·18
Oishinbo
Tetsu Kariya · 111 vols
▸ Cultural Integrity · Authenticity · Craft

A food journalist argues for authentic Japanese cuisine against commercialization. Reflect: What cultural practices am I watching become commodified — and what is lost?

Anime II·19
Ghost in the Shell
Oshii · Film · 1995
▸ Identity · Consciousness · What Makes a Self

A cyborg policewoman asks if she has a soul. Reflect: What do I consider the essential core of "me"? What would survive the loss of my body?

Manga II·20
Biomega
Tsutomu Nihei · 6 vols
▸ Post-human Ethics · Loyalty · Survival

A posthuman agent and a synthetic bear navigate a collapsed world. Reflect: What loyalties would I keep if society completely collapsed?

Anime II·21
Dororo
Series · 2019
▸ Sacrifice · Agency · Parental Sin

A lord trades his son's body parts to demons for land. The son reclaims them one by one. Reflect: What have others sacrificed without my knowledge to give me what I have?

Manga II·22
Liar Game
Shinobu Kaitani · 19 vols
▸ Trust · Betrayal · Collective Action Problems

Players compete in psychological games designed to make them betray each other. Reflect: Under what conditions do I stop trusting people — and how do I rebuild?

Anime II·23
Akira
Otomo · Film · 1988
▸ Power Corruption · Youth Rage · Urban Collapse

Unchecked psychic power destroys a city and a friendship. Reflect: What happens when someone without wisdom is given enormous power?

Manga II·24
Akira
Katsuhiro Otomo · 6 vols
▸ Government Secrecy · Human Experimentation · Rebellion

The manga expands on the state's role in creating — and hiding — its monsters. Reflect: What does my government hide from me in the name of security?

Anime II·25
Neon Genesis Evangelion
Series · 1995
▸ Trauma · Self-worth · Instrumentalizing Others

A boy is asked to save the world but cannot even connect with the people next to him. Reflect: Have I ever used someone as a tool for my own salvation?

III — Grief & Impermanence 25 works

"Nothing lasts. What do we do with that?" Works on loss, death, memory, and the strange grace that grief sometimes brings.

Anime III·01
Your Lie in April
Shigatsu wa Kimi no Uso · Series · 2014
▸ Grief · Music · Letting Go

A pianist who stopped hearing music meets a violinist who is dying. Reflect: What grief have I been avoiding through numbness? What music am I not allowing myself to hear?

Manga III·02
Your Lie in April
Naoshi Arakawa · 11 vols
▸ Living with Terminal Illness · Gift-giving

The manga lingers on what a dying person chooses to leave behind. Reflect: What would I want to leave? What do I want to make sure someone knows before it's too late?

Anime III·03
Anohana: The Flower We Saw That Day
Series · 2011
▸ Unresolved Grief · Friendship · Guilt Survival

A group of childhood friends scattered by a friend's death reunite to fulfill her wish. Reflect: Is there an unfinished grief I've been avoiding by staying away from something?

Manga III·04
Goodnight Punpun
Inio Asano · 13 vols
▸ Depression · Trauma · Coming of Age

An unflinching portrait of a boy's psychological disintegration from childhood to adulthood. Reflect: What childhood wounds did I normalize that I've been carrying into adult behavior?

Anime III·05
Hotarubi no Mori e
Film · 2011
▸ Longing · Ephemeral Love · Beauty of Limits

A girl loves a forest spirit who will disappear if touched. Reflect: What relationships in my life am I loving poorly because I'm afraid of their ending?

Manga III·06
Solanin
Inio Asano · 2 vols
▸ Sudden Loss · Meaning · Young Adulthood

A young couple navigate listlessness until death forces a reckoning with what matters. Reflect: Am I waiting for a crisis to clarify what I actually value?

Anime III·07
The Tale of Princess Kaguya
Isao Takahata · Film · 2013
▸ Mortality · Nature · Desire and Loss

A celestial being lives a full human life she never asked for, then must leave it. Reflect: What is the relationship between desire and suffering? Would I choose mortality?

Manga III·08
Ikigami: The Ultimate Limit
Motoro Mase · 10 vols
▸ State Death · Meaning · Last Hours

Citizens are randomly selected at birth to die between 18–24; a civil servant delivers the notices. Reflect: If I had 24 hours left, what would I do? What does that reveal about how I'm living now?

Anime III·09
Death Parade
Series · 2015
▸ Judgment · What Lives Reveal · Fairness

Dead souls are judged through bar games that reveal their true character. Reflect: How would I be judged? What does my behavior reveal about who I really am?

Manga III·10
A Walk to Remember (Manga Adaptation)

▸ Terminal Illness · First Love · Faith

A teenager falls in love with a dying girl whose faith he doesn't share. Reflect: Can love transform who I am without requiring me to share the other's beliefs?

Anime III·11
Angel Beats!
Series · 2010
▸ Unresolved Life · Acceptance · Moving On

Dead teenagers in a purgatory school rebel against disappearing. Reflect: What am I refusing to let go of? What would it mean to complete something?

Manga III·12
Children of the Sea
Daisuke Igarashi · 5 vols
▸ Ocean · Time · Cosmic Scale of Life

A girl meets boys who were raised by the sea and may return to it. Reflect: How does thinking at planetary or oceanic scale change how I relate to my own existence?

Anime III·13
Millennium Actress

▸ Longing · Memory · A Life Defined by Pursuit

An actress spent her entire life chasing a man she met once. Reflect: What have I organized my entire life around that I've never questioned?

Manga III·14
The Flowers of Evil
Shuzo Oshimi · 11 vols
▸ Shame · Desire · Adolescent Alienation

A bookish teenager is blackmailed by the class outcast into increasingly transgressive acts. Reflect: What shameful part of myself have I never looked at directly?

Anime III·15
Colorful
Film · 2010
▸ Suicide · Family · Being Seen

A soul inhabits the body of a boy who attempted suicide and slowly learns what he missed. Reflect: What about the life I have do I take for granted that others would treasure?

V — Belonging & Identity 25 works

"Who am I among others?" Works on gender, community, the in-between, the outsider, and the many selves we carry.

Anime V·01
Revolutionary Girl Utena
Series · 1997
▸ Gender Performance · Power in Relationships · Myth

A girl who wants to be a prince destabilizes every gender assumption in her academy. Reflect: What roles have I accepted without choosing them? Which would I keep if I could choose freely?

Manga V·02
Wandering Son (Hourou Musuko)
Shimura Takako · 8 vols
▸ Gender Identity · Adolescence · Vulnerability

Two children — one born male who wants to be a girl, one born female who wants to be a boy — navigate adolescence with uncommon tenderness. Reflect: What do I not understand about gender identity — and what would understanding cost me?

Anime V·03
Wandering Son
Series · 2011
▸ Peer Cruelty · Identity Formation

The adaptation is visually exquisite — a watercolor world for a tender story. Reflect: How did I react to children who were "different" when I was young? How do I react now?

Manga V·04
Dungeon Meshi
(see Section II cross-reference)
▸ Cultural Identity through Food

Different species with different food cultures must learn to cook for each other. A parable for multicultural respect.

Anime V·05
The Ancient Magus' Bride
Series · 2017
▸ Self-worth · Belonging · Chosen Family

A girl who has been sold finds herself in a world of magic and slowly learns she has value. Reflect: What early messages about my own worth do I still believe without questioning?

Manga V·06
The Ancient Magus' Bride
Kore Yamazaki · ongoing
▸ Folklore Identity · Self-construction

The manga is richer in British folklore — each creature is a way of being human. Reflect: What myths does my culture use to explain human types? Are those myths limiting?

Anime V·07
Shirobako
Series · 2014
▸ Creative Work · Vocation · Workplace Community

Five friends pursue careers in anime production and discover the gap between dreams and labor. Reflect: Is what I work toward actually what I want — or a younger self's dream I haven't revisited?

Manga V·08
Genshiken
Shimoku Kio · 9 vols
▸ Subculture Identity · Belonging · Authenticity

An otaku club navigates identity, outsider culture, and what it means to belong somewhere. Reflect: What communities do I belong to that others dismiss? What does that community give me that mainstream culture doesn't?

Anime V·09
Banana Fish
Series · 2018
▸ Abuse · Chosen Family · Love without Labels

A gang leader and a Japanese photographer forge one of fiction's most intense bonds. Reflect: What does profound love look like when it resists easy labeling?

Manga V·10
Banana Fish
Akimi Yoshida · 19 vols
▸ Childhood Trauma · Street Survival · Drug Politics
