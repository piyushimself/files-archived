About This Encyclopedia

This document is the complete ratings record for every film mentioned across all movie PDFs produced in this session — a total of 647 unique titles. The previous ratings PDF covered only 218 films; this corrected edition covers all 647, including the

director filmographies from the Masters of Cinema by Genre reference. Films are grouped by their source PDF or section, then sorted alphabetically within each group. The final section ranks all films

by IMDB score so you can see where everything stands across the complete session. IMDB scores (out of 10) reflect weighted audience votes — more votes produce more stable scores. Rotten Tomatoes Tomatometer (%) reflects the proportion of professional critics giving a positive review. For films with fewer than 20 RT reviews, scores are marked with an asterisk (*) in the notes column. Some extremely obscure or regional films may show RT

scores based on limited data. Note: Where the same director's film appears in multiple sections (e.g. Grave of the Fireflies in both '100 Essential Films' and
