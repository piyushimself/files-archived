Critically Acclaimed · Mature · Meaningful

A curated reference guide to the finest films adapted from manga — spanning anime classics, live-action adaptations, and global cinema from 1972 through 2025. Compiled for serious film journaling and self-discovery

PAGE 2

How to Use This Guide This guide lists 500 films adapted from manga — the Japanese art form of comics and graphic novels. It is organized into five sections: Anime Films (Sections I & IV), Live-Action Japanese Adaptations (Section II), International Adaptations (Section III), and an Extended Catalog (Section V). Films marked with a director name in italics indicate the director also created the source manga. Films appearing more than once are foundational works considered essential enough to mention repeatedly. Use this as a watchlist, a reference, or a self-discovery journal companion — each film asks you something different about who you are. Start anywhere. Follow your instincts. Write about what you watch. The best use of this list is not to complete it — but to let it guide you somewhere unexpected.
