Ek Inqilaabi Kavita Yatra

"Sabse khatarnak hota hai hamari hi sapnon ka mar jaana" — Paash (Avtar Singh Sandhu)

PAGE 2

Ek Baat

Paash — Avtar Singh Sandhu — ne Punjabi kavita ko ek naya chehra diya. Unki kavita 'Sabse Khatarnak' ek esi awaaz hai jo dil ke andar tak dhansati hai. Woh kavita sirf shabd nahin, woh ek andolan hai. Ek esi pukaar jo aaj bhi utni hi praasgik hai jitni pehle thi. Yeh sangrah usi parampara mein likhaa gaya hai — us jazbe mein, us aag mein, us sachchi takleef aur umeed mein. Yahan 100 kavitaen hain jo insaan ke dard, sapne, sangharsh, aur mohabbat ki baat karti hain. Hindi mein, saral bhasha mein, par dil ke gehre andar se. Yeh kavitaen un logon ke liye hain jo abhi bhi maan te hain ke duniya badal sakti hai. Jo abhi bhi sapne dekhte hain. Jo abhi bhi ladhte hain. Jo abhi bhi jeete hain — sach mein.

Sabse Khatarnak

— Paash — Mehnat ki loot sabse khatarnak nahin hoti Police ki maar sabse khatarnak nahin hoti Gaddari, lobh ki maar sabse khatarnak nahin hoti Sabse khatarnak hota hai Hamari hi sapnon ka mar jaana Aankhon ka bojhal ho jaana Roshni se dar jaana Sabse khatarnak woh ghari hoti hai Teri chhati pe chalni jab Ullu ki taraf se aa ke lagti hai goli Woh jo tujhe andar se maar deti hai Sabse khatarnak hota hai Murdaaghar mein baith ke sapne dekhna Woh neend jo sab se gehri hoti hai Jinhe sone do — unhe sone do
