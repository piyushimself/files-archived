100 poems · Original sequence preserved · Search by poet or section
About this collection

A 100-poem Hindi collection in a revolutionary and humanist tradition, with poems on resistance, dignity, labour, hope, identity and love. Each poem is presented as its own piece, with the source title, poet attribution, verse lineation and stanza breaks preserved.

Suggested approach: Read one poem at a time. Poem titles, poet credits, verse line breaks and stanza spacing are retained; use the poet filter or search to find a particular voice.

COLLECTION NOTES · SOURCE PAGE 2

Ek Baat

Paash — Avtar Singh Sandhu — ne Punjabi kavita ko ek naya

chehra diya. Unki kavita 'Sabse Khatarnak' ek esi awaaz hai jo dil ke

andar tak dhansati hai. Woh kavita sirf shabd nahin, woh ek andolan

hai. Ek esi pukaar jo aaj bhi utni hi praasgik hai jitni pehle thi.

Yeh sangrah usi parampara mein likhaa gaya hai — us jazbe mein,

us aag mein, us sachchi takleef aur umeed mein. Yahan 100

kavitaen hain jo insaan ke dard, sapne, sangharsh, aur mohabbat ki

baat karti hain. Hindi mein, saral bhasha mein, par dil ke gehre

andar se.

Yeh kavitaen un logon ke liye hain jo abhi bhi maan te hain ke

duniya badal sakti hai. Jo abhi bhi sapne dekhte hain. Jo abhi bhi

ladhte hain. Jo abhi bhi jeete hain — sach mein.

Sabse Khatarnak

Paash

Mehnat ki loot sabse khatarnak nahin hoti

Police ki maar sabse khatarnak nahin hoti

Gaddari, lobh ki maar sabse khatarnak nahin hoti

Sabse khatarnak hota hai

Hamari hi sapnon ka mar jaana

Aankhon ka bojhal ho jaana

Roshni se dar jaana

Sabse khatarnak woh ghari hoti hai

Teri chhati pe chalni jab

Ullu ki taraf se aa ke lagti hai goli

Woh jo tujhe andar se maar deti hai

Sabse khatarnak hota hai

Murdaaghar mein baith ke sapne dekhna

Woh neend jo sab se gehri hoti hai

Jinhe sone do — unhe sone do

Zinda Rehna

Gorakh Pandey

Zinda rehne ke liye

Sirf sans lena kaafi nahin hota

Dil mein koi aag chahiye

Jo andheron ko jalaye

Zinda rehna matlab

Kisi ke dard ko apna maanna

Kisi ke aansu ko

Apni aankh se bahana

Jo sirf apne liye jeete hain

Woh marte hain baar baar

Jo doosron ke liye jeete hain

Woh kabhi nahin marte

Inquilaab

Dhumil

Inquilaab ka matlab sirf naara nahin

Inquilaab ka matlab hai

Har roz uthna aur ladna

Khud se, duniya se, waqt se

Inquilaab woh bhi hai

Jab ek aurat pehli baar

Apna naam lete hue nahin darti

Jab ek bachcha sawaal karta hai

Aur usse chup nahin karaya jaata

Har chhoti jeet ek inquilaab hai

Har sach bolne ki himmat

Ek inquilaab hai

Nayi Subah

Gulzar

POEM 36SOURCE PAGE 43

Shahar
