Mind Detox

200 Writing Prompts

A sequential writing course for quieting mental noise — moving through six phases: unloading what crowds the mind, quieting through sensory attention, releasing what you have been carrying, clarifying what actually matters, restoring what nourishes, and finally returning to yourself. Each

prompt includes guidance on how to approach it. Work through the phases in order — the sequence matters. Prompt 100 is a midpoint check-in.
