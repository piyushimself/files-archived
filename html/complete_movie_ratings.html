About These Ratings

This document compiles every film recommended across the three movie PDFs produced in this session — a total of 218 unique titles. Each is listed with its IMDb score (out of 10, based on user votes) and its Rotten Tomatoes Tomatometer score

(percentage of positive critical reviews). IMDb scores reflect popular audience consensus weighted by vote count — higher-voted films have more stable scores. Rotten Tomatoes Tomatometer reflects the percentage of professional critics who gave a film a positive review — it does not measure how positive, only whether positive or negative. A film can have 100% RT with mostly lukewarm positive reviews, while a film

with 70% may have deeply passionate defenders. For older and more obscure films — particularly world cinema, avant-garde, and some erotic art cinema titles — RT scores may be based on fewer than 20 reviews and should be treated as directional rather than definitive. IMDb scores for older films are

often lower than their critical reputation because fewer general users have seen them. Films are grouped by their source PDF, then sorted alphabetically within each section. The colour coding allows quick

identification of critical consensus at a glance. Complete Movie Ratings: IMDB & Rotten Tomatoes | All Films From This Session | Page 2
