INTRODUCTION

This is a course on death — not a morbid exercise, but a considered attempt to engage with the one certainty

of existence through the greatest art humanity has made about it. Arranged in seven thematic units, each moving through a different angle of mortality, the course pairs literary works with films, musical compositions, and audio material. Every unit can be approached independently, but taken together they form a complete education in what it means to be a creature who

knows it will die. The works selected span centuries and cultures: Tolstoy and Tarkovsky, Marcus Aurelius and Nick Cave, Dante and Pixar. Ancient wisdom alongside contemporary grief. Each entry carries a format tag — READ,

WATCH, or LISTEN — and a curatorial note on why it belongs and what it offers. READ Books, poetry, essays, memoirs, philosophy WATCH Films, series, documentary LISTEN Music, podcasts, audiobooks, lectures

UNIT I

Grief, Loss & the Wound That Stays How the living are haunted. The private archaeology of losing someone — and the strange, persistent ways

we carry the dead. "Grief is the price we pay for love."

— QUEEN ELIZABETH II

READ

A Grief Observed — C.S. Lewis

1961 · MEMOIR / ESSAY

Lewis's raw journal kept after his wife's death. Ruthlessly honest about faith, rage, and the unreliability of

memory.

READ

The Year of Magical Thinking — Joan Didion

2005 · MEMOIR

The finest modern memoir on grief. Didion charts the delusions the mind constructs to refuse death.

READ

Levels of Life — Julian Barnes

2013 · ESSAY / MEMOIR

Three-part meditation on ballooning, photography, and widowhood — one of the most precise books ever

written on loss.

WATCH

Ordinary People (1980)

DIR. ROBERT REDFORD · DRAMA

A family fracturing after the death of a son. Quiet, devastating, and criminally underrated.

WATCH

Manchester by the Sea (2016)

DIR. KENNETH LONERGAN · DRAMA

Grief as paralysis. A man trapped in a past he cannot outrun. Devastatingly restrained.

WATCH

Aftersun (2022)

DIR. CHARLOTTE WELLS · DRAMA

Memory, absence, and a father's hidden despair. What we don't see is what destroys us.

LISTEN

Sufjan Stevens — Carrie & Lowell

2015 · ALBUM · FOLK / INDIE

An album-length elegy for his mother. Sparse, haunted, and deeply liturgical in its grief.

LISTEN

Terrible, Thanks for Asking — Nora McInerny

PODCAST · ONGOING

Real stories of loss, told without sanitizing. The most honest audio space on grief in popular culture.

UNIT II

The Art of Dying Well The deathbed as a moral and spiritual theater. How humans approach their own end — with terror, dignity,

resignation, or grace. "It is not death that a man should fear, but he should fear never beginning to live."

— MARCUS AURELIUS

READ

The Death of Ivan Ilyich — Leo Tolstoy

1886 · NOVELLA

The essential literary text on dying. A man of conventional success confronts the meaninglessness of his life as

death approaches. Short; devastating.

READ

When Breath Becomes Air — Paul Kalanithi

2016 · MEMOIR

A neurosurgeon diagnosed with terminal cancer turns to literature and asks what makes a life worth living.

Profound and beautifully written.

READ

Being Mortal — Atul Gawande

2014 · NONFICTION

A surgeon's case for why medicine must reckon with mortality rather than fight it at all costs.

WATCH

Ikiru (1952)

DIR. AKIRA KUROSAWA · DRAMA

A bureaucrat given months to live seeks meaning. Kurosawa's most humane film and one of the great works of

world cinema.

WATCH

The Savages (2007)

DIR. TAMARA JENKINS · DRAMA

Two adult siblings navigate placing their aging father in a nursing home. Funny, achingly honest, and

unsparing.

LISTEN

Death, Sex & Money — Anna Sale

PODCAST · WNYC STUDIOS

Long-form conversations about the hard parts of life — including terminal illness, end-of-life decisions, and

legacy.

LISTEN

Nick Cave — Skeleton Tree

2016 · ALBUM · POST-ROCK / ART

Recorded during and after the death of Cave's teenage son. A document of the impossible task of making art

while grief is still wet. ✦✦✦

UNIT III

Afterlives, Limbo & What Comes Next

Heaven, hell, purgatory, reincarnation, nothingness — and the stories cultures tell to make peace with the

unknown. "Death is not an event in life: we do not live to experience death."

— LUDWIG WITTGENSTEIN

READ

The Divine Comedy — Dante Alighieri

~1320 · EPIC POEM

The architecture of the medieval afterlife. Start with the Hollander or Ciardi translation. Inferno alone is a

masterpiece.

READ

Sum: Forty Tales from the Afterlives — David Eagleman

2009 · SPECULATIVE FICTION

Forty radically different visions of what happens after death. Playful, melancholy, and philosophically rich.

READ

Lincoln in the Bardo — George Saunders

2017 · NOVEL

Ghosts refusing to pass on, a grieving president, and a meditation on attachment. Formally wild; emotionally

overwhelming.

WATCH

Coco (2017)

PIXAR · ANIMATED

The Mexican Dia de los Muertos tradition brought to gorgeous life. One of cinema's most loving treatments of

remembrance.

WATCH

A Matter of Life and Death (1946)

DIR. POWELL & PRESSBURGER · FANTASY

A pilot between worlds argues for his life before a celestial court. Staggeringly beautiful and philosophically

playful.

WATCH

Nostalghia (1983)

DIR. ANDREI TARKOVSKY · DRAMA

The liminal space between worlds — a poet's journey through fog, water, and light. Slow cinema at its most

transcendent.

LISTEN

Radiolab — "After Life" Episodes

PODCAST · WNYC

Scientific and philosophical investigations of what death is, when it happens, and whether anything persists.

LISTEN

Requiem in D Minor, K.626 — Mozart

1791 · CLASSICAL / CHORAL

Left unfinished at his death. The Lacrimosa may be the most moving two minutes in Western music. Use the

Gardiner recording.

UNIT IV

War, Mass Death & Collective Mourning

When death comes wholesale. Literature and film struggling to make meaning of industrialized killing and

the grief of entire generations. "So it goes."

— KURT VONNEGUT, SLAUGHTERHOUSE-FIVE

READ

All Quiet on the Western Front — Erich Maria Remarque

1929 · NOVEL

The definitive anti-war novel. A young German soldier watches his generation obliterated. Read it before

watching any war film.

READ

The Book Thief — Markus Zusak

2005 · NOVEL

Narrated by Death himself. WWII Germany seen through a girl who steals books. Death as a weary, reluctant

witness.

READ

Slaughterhouse-Five — Kurt Vonnegut

1969 · NOVEL

"So it goes." Vonnegut's refrain, repeated at every death, becomes the novel's moral argument. Absurdist and

heartbroken.

WATCH

Come and See (1985)

DIR. ELEM KLIMOV · WAR DRAMA

The most devastating war film ever made. A boy's face documents what mass death does to human

consciousness.

WATCH

Son of Saul (2015)

DIR. LASZLO NEMES · DRAMA

A Sonderkommando at Auschwitz. Shot in extreme close-up; the horror exists in peripheral blur. An ethical

masterpiece.

LISTEN

War Requiem — Benjamin Britten

1962 · ORCHESTRAL / CHORAL

The Latin Mass for the Dead interwoven with Wilfred Owen's WWI poetry. A monumental meditation on war

and sacrifice.

LISTEN

Hardcore History — "Blueprint for Armageddon"

PODCAST · DAN CARLIN · 6 PARTS

Six episodes on WWI's industrial slaughter. The most immersive audio history ever produced. Essential

preparation for the literature. ✦✦✦

UNIT V

The Death of Children & the Young The death considered most unjust. How art grapples with lives cut before flowering — and the particular

grief of those left behind. "No parents should have to bury their child."

— THEODEN, THE LORD OF THE RINGS

READ

The Lovely Bones — Alice Sebold

2002 · NOVEL

A murdered girl narrates her family's grief from heaven. The novel's radical perspective reshapes how we

think about death and witness.

READ

A Monster Calls — Patrick Ness

2011 · YA NOVEL

A boy whose mother is dying is visited by a monster demanding truth. One of the most emotionally honest

books about anticipatory grief.

READ

Kindertotenlied — Friedrich Ruckert

1872 · POETRY

Poems written after the death of two children. Raw paternal grief that Mahler later set to music. Read

alongside the song cycle.

WATCH

Grave of the Fireflies (1988)

DIR. ISAO TAKAHATA · ANIMATED

Two orphaned children in WWII Japan. Perhaps the saddest film ever made. Anime as high tragedy.

WATCH

About Time (2013)

DIR. RICHARD CURTIS · DRAMA / FANTASY

Time travel as a meditation on presence, loss, and the value of unrepeatable moments with those we love.

LISTEN

Kindertotenlieder — Gustav Mahler

1904 · SONG CYCLE · CLASSICAL

Five songs on the death of children, set for voice and orchestra. Use the Kathleen Ferrier or Janet Baker

recording. Inconsolable. ✦✦✦

UNIT VI

Philosophy of Death & Mortality The thinkers who stared longest. From Stoic acceptance to existentialist defiance to Buddhist dissolution of

the self. "Death is nothing to us, since when we exist, death is not yet present, and when death is present, then we do not exist."

— EPICURUS

READ

Meditations — Marcus Aurelius

~170 CE · PHILOSOPHY

The Stoic emperor's private journal. "Death smiles at us all; all a man can do is smile back." Use the Gregory

Hays translation.

READ

The Denial of Death — Ernest Becker

1973 · PHILOSOPHY / PSYCHOLOGY

Pulitzer Prize winner. All human culture and civilization is, Becker argues, a response to the terror of death.

A singular book.

READ

Staring at the Sun — Irvin D. Yalom

2008 · EXISTENTIAL PSYCHOTHERAPY

A psychiatrist on overcoming death anxiety. The most practical philosophical guide to mortality in the modern

canon.

READ

The Tibetan Book of the Dead

8TH CENTURY · SPIRITUAL TEXT

A guide to the dying and the dead, read aloud to ease the transition. A completely different civilizational

relationship to death.

WATCH

The Seventh Seal (1957)

DIR. INGMAR BERGMAN · DRAMA

A knight plays chess with Death. Bergman's most iconic film — a direct confrontation with meaninglessness

and the silence of God.

LISTEN

Shelly Kagan — "Death" Yale Open Course

PODCAST / LECTURE SERIES · YALE UNIVERSITY

Yale philosopher Shelly Kagan's open course on death is freely available online. Start from lecture one and

do not stop.

LISTEN

Four Last Songs — Richard Strauss

1948 · CLASSICAL / SOPRANO

Composed at 84, Strauss's farewell to life. "Im Abendrot" ends with two larks ascending. Use the Jessye

Norman recording. ✦✦✦

SOURCE PAGE 10

UNIT VII

Dark Comedy & Death's Absurdity Laughing at the abyss. The tradition of finding in death not only tragedy but farce — and how humor has

always been a survival strategy. "Always look on the bright side of death."

— MONTY PYTHON'S LIFE OF BRIAN

READ

Death with Interruptions — Jose Saramago

2005 · NOVEL

Death stops working in a small country. A satirical exploration of what mortality actually provides to

civilization and meaning.

READ

The Vegetarian — Han Kang

2007 · NOVEL

A woman's body as a site of death-drive and refusal. Disturbing, surreal, and unlike anything else in

contemporary fiction.

READ

Slaughterhouse-Five — Kurt Vonnegut

1969 · NOVEL

Revisited here for its comedy: Vonnegut's "So it goes" is one of literature's great absurdist inventions. Laugh

and weep.

WATCH

Harold and Maude (1971)

DIR. HAL ASHBY · DARK COMEDY

A death-obsessed boy befriends a 79-year-old woman who loves being alive. The greatest argument for

embracing mortality ever put on film.

WATCH

The Death of Stalin (2017)

DIR. ARMANDO IANNUCCI · DARK COMEDY

Power, fear, and political death rendered as farce. Shows how the machinery of death makes fools of

everyone around it.

WATCH

Six Feet Under (2001–2005)

HBO SERIES · DRAMA / DARK COMEDY

Every episode begins with a death. A funeral home family as lens on mortality, identity, and what it means to

actually be alive.

LISTEN

The Reith Lectures — "Mortal Coil" — Jarvis Cocker

BBC RADIO 4 · 2023

The Pulp frontman's surprising and genuinely wise BBC lectures on death in popular culture, music, and

everyday life.

LISTEN

Ars Moriendi (Medieval Manual on Dying)

AUDIOBOOK · 15TH CENTURY

Medieval guidance on how to die well. Listen alongside Gawande's Being Mortal for its deep historical echo.
