Free Art & Drawing Resource Library

A curated, free, no-signup-required list — drawing fundamentals through comics, storytelling, and a Kleon-style creative input diet. Compiled July 2026.

1. Drawing Fundamentals

Drawabox — https://drawabox.com/ — Structured, exercise-based construction drawing curriculum. Best starting point for anyone, any style.

Proko — https://www.proko.com/ — Sequenced lessons: gesture, construction, anatomy, portraiture. Also on YouTube (@ProkoTV).

Ctrl+Paint — https://ctrlpaint.com/ — Free digital painting fundamentals — values, color, brush control — organized foundation to advanced.

2. Anatomy & Figure Practice

Line of Action — https://line-of-action.com/ — Free timed figure-drawing practice tool (30 sec to 10 min poses) — the practice gym to pair with Proko's theory.

3. Painting & Color Theory

Draw Mix Paint — https://drawmixpaint.com/ — Free structured video series on oil painting realism, color mixing, and value — atelier-style progression.

Ctrl+Paint — https://ctrlpaint.com/ — Also covers color/value fundamentals, medium-agnostic (works for digital, gouache, etc.).

Hue, Value, Chroma — — Dedicated free color theory reference site — good companion once you're mixing paint.

Jerry's Artarama — free watercolor lessons — https://www.jerrysartarama.com/free-art-instruction-videos/medium/watercolors — Free tutorial library (not a full sequenced course — Watercolor College is the paid, complete equivalent).

4. Charcoal

Jerry's Artarama — free charcoal lessons — https://www.jerrysartarama.com/free-art-instruction-videos/medium/charcoal — Free but topic-scattered, not a sequence.

Note: no complete free structured charcoal curriculum exists online. Charcoal is largely the same skills as graphite/Proko form study, applied with a different tool — treat it as a medium variation rather than a separate discipline.

5. Perspective & Urban Sketching

Circle Line Art School — https://www.youtube.com/@circlelineartschool — Tom Wood's channel — perspective fundamentals through to buildings, streets, and urban scenes. Closest free 'Drawabox for urban sketching.'

Urban Sketchers (USk) — https://urbansketchers.org/ — The global urban sketching movement — manifesto, regional chapter blogs, real process breakdowns, local meetups.

Tip: do Drawabox's perspective lessons first, then apply via Circle Line's building-specific lessons.

6. Illustration, Character Design & Storytelling

SVS Learn — https://svslearn.com/ — Free tier available. Built by working children's book illustrators — concept, composition, color, narrative.

7. Art History

Smarthistory — https://smarthistory.org/ — 4,000+ free resources organized into real syllabi (World Art History, Renaissance to Modern, Ancient Greek Art, etc). No ads, no paywall.

Khan Academy Art History — https://www.khanacademy.org/humanities/art-history — Free structured video/article courses, produced in partnership with Smarthistory.

8. Manga & Anime

Mark Crilley — https://www.youtube.com/@markcrilley — Hundreds of free step-by-step manga/anime tutorials, beginner-friendly.

Anime Art Academy — https://animeartacademy.com/ — Free starter lesson with real red-line feedback from working manga artists.

Envato Tuts+ — Anime Anatomy — https://design.tutsplus.com/tutorials/how-to-draw-anime-characters--cms-31864 — Free written tutorial bridging real anatomy and anime proportions.

Internet Archive — How to Draw Manga — https://archive.org/details/howtodrawmanga00soci — Scanned classic Japanese instructional reference.

9. Comics Craft & Structured Storytelling

The Nearsighted Monkey — https://thenearsightedmonkey.tumblr.com/ — Lynda Barry's own Tumblr — her actual UW-Madison 'Making Comics' class assignments, posted in real time.

Spin Weave and Cut — https://spinweaveandcut.com/making-comics-f2018/ — A full free 'Making Comics' syllabus with week-by-week exercises and PDFs, modeled on Barry's course.

Scott McCloud — https://scottmccloud.com/ — Free essays and comics excerpts on how comics work as a form — panel transitions, closure, cartoon faces.

Matt Madden — — Search 'Matt Madden 99 Ways to Tell a Story' — constraint-based comic-making exercises and games, free on his blog.

10. Kleon-Style Creative Input Diet

Austin Kleon — Newsletter — https://austinkleon.com/newsletter/ — Weekly '10 things worth sharing' — the clearest artifact of his own input habit.

Austin Kleon — Blog — https://austinkleon.com/blog/ — His daily reading/noticing log. Browse in small doses, don't binge.

Danny Gregory — Blog — https://dannygregorysblog.com/ — Built for exactly the 'used to draw, lost the habit' re-entry moment.

Danny Gregory — Newsletter — https://dannysessays.com/ — Free weekly essays on creativity and drawing.

Danny Gregory — Website / YouTube — https://www.dannygregory.com/ — Video essays and sketchbook tours, 500k+ subscribers.

Wendy MacNaughton — https://wendymacnaughton.com/ — Illustrated journalism from plain daily observation.

Sarah Andersen — Sarah's Scribbles — — On Instagram/Webtoon — mundane personal moments turned into a few honest panels.

Gemma Correll — https://gemmacorrell.com/ — Similar territory to Sarah Andersen, more absurdist.

How to actually use this list

Consume in small daily doses (newsletter + one blog post), not binge sessions.

Pair every input session with one small output — a single page, even badly drawn.

Keep a running 'commonplace book' of overheard lines and small daily moments, as raw material for later comics.

Sequence for drawing fundamentals: Drawabox, then Proko, then Line of Action for practice, then Ctrl+Paint or Draw Mix Paint for color.

Sequence for comics re-entry: Kleon newsletter and Danny Gregory blog daily, one page a day, no theme required.
