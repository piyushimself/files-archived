The Emotional Journey

Innocence · Wound · Grief · Rage · Numbness · Healing · Gratitude · Wonder A complete passage through the full spectrum of human feeling. The Philosophical Arc

Innocence · Awakening · Suffering · Searching · Doubt · Acceptance · Transcendence A curriculum for the thinking mind and the questioning soul. These films do not answer — they deepen the questions. Follow the arc of a mind moving from naive certainty through doubt, darkness, and searching toward a hard-won, unsentimental peace.

The Human Lifecycle

Birth · Childhood · Youth · Love · Work · Marriage · Parenthood · Ageing · Death A complete human life told in 50 films. Each film represents a stage of existence — chosen not for plot but for the emotional truth of that life-stage. Watch them in order over months or years. ON COMPLETING THE CURRICULUM

After Curriculum 1 — The Emotional Journey

You will be able to name your feelings with more precision. You will be slower to flee them. You will have a larger vocabulary for

what it means to be a human being who feels things.

After Curriculum 2 — The Philosophical Arc

You will hold your beliefs more lightly. You will be more comfortable not knowing. You will have sat with the void and found that it

does not consume you.

After Curriculum 3 — The Human Lifecycle

You will see your own life as a stage in a larger arc. You will be more patient with other people's stages. You will understand that

every age has its own specific grief and its own specific beauty. After Curriculum 4 — Chaos to Stillness You will be able to sit with silence. You will notice more. You will be less addicted to stimulation. The world will have slowed slightly

and permanently.

After All Four

You will be a different person than when you began. Not better — different. More porous. More willing to be changed by what you

see. This is what great cinema does. It is what you came for. "Cinema is a mirror by which we often see ourselves." — Martin Scorsese Page 18 · Asian Cinema Curriculum · 4 Arcs × 50 Films · 200 Films Total
