1000 Greatest Fictional Books — International & Regional A Global Reading Life A Note on This List Jorge Luis Borges imagined a Library of Babel containing every book that could possibly be written. This list is the opposite: the books that were actually written, and that actually mattered — to their

cultures, to other writers, to readers across centuries. You will notice that some great works appear multiple times — once in their national tradition, once in a thematic section. This is intentional. The Brothers Karamazov belongs to Russian literature and to world literature. The Tale of Genji belongs to Japanese tradition and to the history of the novel

itself. A great book earns its multiple contexts. Where to begin? Begin with whatever calls to you. If you have read mostly Western literature, start

with the African or Southeast Asian section. If you know the modern novel well, go back to the ancient epics. If you read only in translation, find one book in the original language of your heritage.

The library is everywhere. You have already begun. — 57 —
