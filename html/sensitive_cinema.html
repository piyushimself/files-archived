About this collection

A film collection centred on emotional sensitivity, empathy and difficult human experiences. Brief context alongside the titles helps viewers choose works for reflection rather than simply follow a ranking.

Suggested approach: Use this edition as a viewing reference. Choose a route or theme that interests you, then follow directors, regions, eras or related works rather than trying to complete every title.
