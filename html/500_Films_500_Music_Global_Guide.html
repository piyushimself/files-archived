PART ONE: 500 FILMS

Cinema is philosophy made visible. These films, across cultures and decades, ask the questions

that matter. This list reaches deliberately into every continent — Japanese precision, Iranian

poetry, African resistance, Korean rage, Latin American magic. Watch one slowly. Then another. Let them accumulate into a view of the world.
